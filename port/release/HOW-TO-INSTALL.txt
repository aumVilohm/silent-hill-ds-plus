Silent Hill DS+ for the Anbernic RG DS Plus (ROCKNIXDS)

No game files are included. You need your own copy of Silent Hill (PlayStation).

1. Put your disc image into the "game" folder: the .bin and .cue, or the .zip
   they came in.
2. Run the prepare script. On Windows, double-click prepare.bat. On macOS or
   Linux, open a terminal in this folder and run:  sh prepare.sh
   It checks your image and puts it into the port folder.
3. Copy everything inside the "ports" folder into roms/ports on the console.
4. Restart the console and start SilentHill from Ports.

More, including controls: https://github.com/aumVilohm/silent-hill-ds-plus
